## Short description (80 chars)

## Full description
VaultKey is a password manager that keeps everything on your device —
no account, no cloud, no tracking, no network calls at all.

Your vault is locked with one master password. Every username, password
and note is encrypted with AES-256 before it is stored, with a fresh
random IV for each value. Only you hold the key.

Features:
- Master password lock with brute-force lockout
- Biometric unlock (fingerprint / face)
- Strong password generator with strength meter
- Search, categories and favorites
- Copy to clipboard with 30-second auto-clear
- Auto-lock after inactivity and when the app backgrounds
- Change master password (re-encrypts the whole vault)
- Erase vault (no recovery, by design)

VaultKey starts empty. Add your logins yourself — nothing is ever
pre-filled, sampled or synced.

Your passwords never leave your phone. That is the whole point.

## Privacy policy summary
VaultKey collects no personal data. No account, no servers, no analytics,
no ads, no third-party SDKs that transmit data. All data is stored
encrypted on the user's device only.
